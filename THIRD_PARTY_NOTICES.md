## Ghostscript (AGPL-3.0)

Pagoblin ships `@jspawn/ghostscript-wasm`, an Emscripten build of Ghostscript, as a
static asset in `public/gs/`. Ghostscript is licensed under the GNU Affero General
Public License v3.0. The corresponding source is available at:

- https://github.com/jsscheller/ghostscript-wasm
- https://git.ghostscript.com/?p=ghostscript.git

Pagoblin's own source is available at
https://github.com/Mutawakkil-Yusuf/Pagoblin. If you run a modified version of
Pagoblin as a network service, the AGPL requires you to offer the corresponding
source to its users.

## qpdf (Apache-2.0)

`@jspawn/qpdf-wasm` is an Emscripten build of qpdf. Source:
https://github.com/jsscheller/qpdf-wasm and https://github.com/qpdf/qpdf.

## PDF.js (Apache-2.0)

The PDF renderer and text extractor is Mozilla PDF.js. Source:
https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js. The worker is served from `public/pdfjs/`.

## Tesseract.js and Tesseract OCR (Apache-2.0)

Local OCR uses tesseract.js and the Tesseract OCR engine. Source:
https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js and https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tesseract.
The core licence text is included at `public/ocr/TESSERACT_CORE_LICENSE`.

## Notes

- The `public/pdfjs/`, `public/gs/`, `public/qpdf/` and `public/ocr/` directories
  are generated by `npm run postinstall` from the packages above. They are not
  edited by hand.
- Some packages have transitive dependencies with their own licences. Run
  `npm ls --all` or inspect `node_modules/<package>/LICENSE` for the exact terms.
- If you believe a component is missing from this list, open an issue and it will
  be corrected.
