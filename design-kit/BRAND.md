# Pagoblin brand guide

## One line
Pagoblin is a free, private PDF toolkit. A tiny goblin called Pip lives in your pocket and does the chewing. Files never leave the device.

## Personality
Warm, playful, quietly confident. Cute, never childish. Premium by being clean, not by being serious.

## Pip (mascot)
- A chubby page creature: amber body, folded dog-ear corner (top right), floppy goblin ears with peach insides, a small sprout on the head, big glossy eyes, rosy cheeks, tiny smile with one fang.
- Files: `assets/pip-happy.svg` (default), `assets/pip-wow.svg` (compressing, surprised).
- Use Pip on: welcome, home greeting, success, empty states, loading, the website hero and footer band.
- Rules: Pip sits on dark or on cream, never on amber of the same shade. Tilt 4 to 8 degrees max. Never stretch, recolor, add outlines, add clothes or change the face. Do not use Pip on every screen. Tool screens stay calm.
- Moods still to draw: sleepy (empty states) and cheer (arms up). Same shapes, only eyes, mouth and arms change.

## Logo
`assets/logo-lockup.svg` (Pip plus wordmark, cream on dark), `assets/app-icon.svg`, `assets/favicon.svg`. Wordmark is Bricolage Grotesque 800, tight tracking. Convert text to outlines before shipping.

## Colour
Dark first. Warm black ground, amber and orange as energy, cream for cards.
- Ink `#17130F` main background. Surface `#241D16` inputs, nav, quiet cards. Line `#3A2F24` borders.
- Cream `#FFF1DC` hero cards and text on dark. Muted `#C9BBA8` secondary text on dark.
- Amber `#FF9F1C` brand colour, highlights, selected states. Orange `#FF6B2C` primary buttons and the add button.
- Peach `#FFC9A0` and Sky `#B7CCFF` are supporting card colours. Sky is used sparingly so tools stay distinguishable.
- Text on amber, orange, peach, sky and cream is always Ink. Text on Ink and Surface is Cream or Muted.
- Never use purple, gradients, glows, or pure black and white. Colour is flat.

## Type
- Display: Bricolage Grotesque 800 for headlines, card titles and big numbers. Letter spacing -0.03em.
- Body: DM Sans 400, 500, 700 for everything else.
- Scale: 88 / 56 / 36 / 30 / 24 / 20 / 17 / 15 / 13 / 11.

## Shape and layout
- Everything is soft: cards 26 to 32 px radius, buttons and chips fully pill-shaped, thumbnails 16 px.
- Phone screens: 390 x 844, 20 px side margin, 12 px gaps. Floating pill nav bottom left plus a round orange add button bottom right.
- Cards may tilt a few degrees and stack (a back card in amber behind a cream front card). Keep it to one tilted moment per screen.
- Primary action is a full-width orange pill at the bottom. Secondary is a Surface pill with a Line border.
- Touch targets at least 44 px. Never rely on colour alone, always pair with a label or icon.
- Website: max width 1200, 72 px between sections, wrap to a single column on phones.

## Voice
Short, plain, a little funny. Say what happens and what it means for the person.
- Yes: "Drop a PDF to start", "Squeeze it to", "All squeezed!", "Nothing was uploaded. Not one byte."
- Errors stay calm, no jokes: "That file is too big for this device. Try a smaller one."
- No hype words, no jargon, no exclamation marks except on success moments. No emoji. Avoid hyphens in copy.

## Claims we can make
Files are processed on the device, no uploads, no ads, no signup. Do not claim speed, user counts or ratings until measured. Word and PowerPoint conversion is "soon" and needs a server.
